## Overview

Designed the RF front-end board for a 10 MHz to 6 GHz receiver. The design integrates a programmable filter, low-noise amplifier, clock generator and up-converter, with a 0.3 to 6 GHz SDR output.

## Product

The board supports stacking and a shielding enclosure for the DFP-1010E Gigabit Ethernet Radio Direction Finding Bearing Processor, a professional-grade SDR platform.
