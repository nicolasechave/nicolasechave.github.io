01 / About

Engineering with a product mindset.

I am an Electronics Engineer with more than 18 years of experience developing commercial electronic products for professional audio, embedded systems and RF applications.

I currently work as an independent engineering consultant, developing an RF front-end and a new VHF direction-finding antenna control board. My work spans system architecture, circuit design and PCB layout through embedded software and manufacturing support.

Until August 2026, I led Engineering & R&D at Solidyne, combining hands-on hardware development with multidisciplinary team leadership. I bring that product development experience to RF, embedded systems and professional audio projects.
